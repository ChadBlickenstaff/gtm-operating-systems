# Deal Inspection Framework

> Deal outcomes are driven by execution, not effort.

This framework documents a repeatable operating model for improving deal execution by reducing manual work, standardizing workflows, and enabling cross-functional collaboration.

Rather than relying on individual effort, the objective is to design systems that make high-quality execution the default.

📖 **Portfolio Version:** https://chadblickenstaff.io/deal-inspection-framework

---

## Overview

This framework explores:

- Why stalled deals are usually a systems problem rather than a people problem.
- Common sources of execution friction across the sales cycle.
- Principles for designing scalable deal execution workflows.
- Practical lessons from Oracle, Zendesk, HubSpot, and CYGNVS.

---

## Operating Principles

- Execution Over Effort
- CRM Is the Source of Truth
- Automation Reduces Manual Error
- Cross-Functional Alignment Starts Early
- Standardized Workflows Scale Better
- Feedback Drives Continuous Improvement
