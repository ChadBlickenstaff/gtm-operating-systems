# AI-Native Account Prioritization Engine

> Designing a workflow that turns GTM judgment into an operational system.

This case study documents the design, implementation, and evaluation of an AI-enabled account prioritization system built during the AlphaForge × Clay GTM Engineering Bootcamp.

Rather than automating outbound, the objective was to operationalize GTM judgment by making qualification, prioritization, segmentation, and action explicit, repeatable, and auditable.

📖 **Portfolio Version:** https://chadblickenstaff.io/building-an-ai-native-account-prioritization-engine

---

## Overview

This project explores:

- Operationalizing GTM judgment through explicit decision layers.
- Designing an end-to-end account prioritization workflow.
- Separating signal, qualification, prioritization, segmentation, and action.
- Lessons learned from building and testing the system against a real market.
